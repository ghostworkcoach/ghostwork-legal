Privacy Policy

Ghostwork (formerly Ghost Kru). Last updated: November 6, 2026

Short version

Ghostwork's publishing tool only connects to Ghostwork's own TikTok account. We don't collect data about viewers, we don't sell data, and these pages use no cookies, analytics or ads.

What the tool receives from TikTok

When the Ghostwork TikTok account is connected through TikTok Login Kit, the tool receives:

  • basic profile information: account ID, display name, username and profile picture (scopes user.info.basic and user.info.profile)
  • account statistics, such as follower and like counts (scope user.info.stats), and the list of the account's own videos with their view counts (scope video.list). These are used only to show Ghostwork how its own posts are doing.
  • access and refresh tokens that let it upload content to that account (scopes video.upload and video.publish)

How it is used

This information is used only to show which account is connected, to show statistics for Ghostwork's own posts, and to upload or publish Ghostwork's own slideshows and videos, either as drafts in the TikTok inbox or as posts. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is not shared with or sold to anyone.

Where it is stored

Tokens and profile information are stored in a private, self-hosted database that only Ghostwork can log in to. Media files uploaded for posting are stored on the same server, so that TikTok can download them when a post is made.

How long we keep it

We keep it while the TikTok account stays connected. When the account is disconnected, the tool stops using it, and we delete the stored data on request. You can also revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services permissions.

Other people

The tool doesn't collect information about anyone who views, likes or comments on our TikTok posts. TikTok handles that under its own Privacy Policy.

Contact and deletion requests

To ask a question or request deletion, email ghostworkcoach@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
